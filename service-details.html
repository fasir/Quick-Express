Overview

Strategic Overview

Establishing a commercial footprint in the United Arab Emirates provides unprecedented access to the MENA region, world-class logistics infrastructure, 100% foreign ownership, and zero personal income taxation.

Whether selecting a Mainland structure through the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) or an internationally connected Free Zone jurisdiction (IFZA, DMCC, Meydan, RAKEZ), Quick Express Business Solutions ensures full legal compliance, optimal tax structuring, and swift trade license issuance.

Checklist

Required Documents

  • Clear passport copy of all shareholders/directors (minimum 6 months validity)
  • UAE Entry Stamp or Tourist/Residence Visa copy (if currently inside the UAE)
  • High-resolution passport-sized photograph (white background)
  • 3 Preferred company trade name choices in order of preference
  • Brief outline of intended commercial activities
