How to Start a Business in Dubai: The Complete 2026 Guide for Expats

Tariq Al-Mansoor November 05, 2026 7 min read

Dubai has firmly cemented its status as one of the world's most dynamic commercial crossroads. With 100% foreign ownership rights, modern infrastructure, and progressive digital governance, establishing a business here in 2026 is faster and more accessible than ever.

1. Choosing the Right Jurisdiction: Mainland vs. Free Zone

The first and most consequential decision for any entrepreneur is selecting the correct economic jurisdiction. Your choice directly influences who you can trade with, office lease obligations, and visa quotas:

Feature Mainland (DED) Free Zone
Foreign Ownership 100% in 1,000+ activities 100% full foreign ownership
Market Access Unrestricted inside UAE & globally International + Free Zone to Free Zone
Government Tenders Eligible to bid directly Limited or via mainland distributor
Customs Duty Standard 5% import duty 0% customs duty inside the zone

2. Essential Steps to Obtain Your Trade License

  1. Define Your Business Activities: Select from over 2,000+ approved commercial, industrial, or professional codes recognized by the UAE Department of Economy & Tourism.
  2. Trade Name Registration: Reserve an official company name that conforms to UAE regulatory naming conventions (e.g. no religious words or political references).
  3. Initial Approval Certificate: Obtain initial governmental sign-off verifying no objections to the enterprise.
  4. Memorandum of Association (MOA): Draft and notarize your corporate charter either through digital notarization or at the notary public.
  5. Workspace Lease (Ejari / Smart Desk): Register a verified physical lease or virtual office package compliant with your authority.
Pro Tip from Quick Express Advisors

Always ensure your selected trade license activities align with your banking compliance profile. Many first-time founders face bank account delays because their license contains high-risk activities that trigger additional KYC audits.

3. Understanding Corporate Tax (9%) & VAT (5%)

Since the implementation of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, UAE entities must maintain accurate financial books. Key highlights include:

  • 0% Rate: Applied on taxable net profit up to AED 375,000.
  • 9% Standard Rate: Applied on taxable net income exceeding AED 375,000.
  • Small Business Relief: Entities with revenue under AED 3,000,000 can elect for tax relief treatment through December 31, 2026.

Conclusion

Launching a business in Dubai provides unprecedented access to global trade. Working alongside an experienced corporate setup partner ensures that name reservations, licensing, residence visas, and corporate banking occur without expensive missteps.
