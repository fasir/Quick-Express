UAE Mainland & Free Zone Company Formation

Comprehensive end-to-end licensing, registration, documentation, and corporate setup support designed for international investors and SMEs.

Strategic Overview

Establishing a commercial footprint in the United Arab Emirates provides unprecedented access to the MENA region, world-class logistics infrastructure, 100% foreign ownership, and zero personal income taxation.

Whether selecting a Mainland structure through the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) or an internationally connected Free Zone jurisdiction (IFZA, DMCC, Meydan, RAKEZ), Quick Express Business Solutions ensures full legal compliance, optimal tax structuring, and swift trade license issuance.

Step-by-Step Formation Process

1
Initial Consultation & Activity Selection

Our legal advisors determine the optimal jurisdiction and business activity codes tailored to your operational model.

2
Trade Name Reservation & Initial Approval

We secure government trade name clearance and initial ministry approval within 24 hours.

3
Document Drafting & MOA Signing

Preparation of Articles of Association (AOA) and Memorandum of Association (MOA) with remote or in-person notarization.

4
License Issuance & Corporate Banking

Official Trade License handed over, followed immediately by Establishment Card, VIP Visa processing, and bank account onboarding.

Required Documents

  • Clear passport copy of all shareholders/directors (minimum 6 months validity)
  • UAE Entry Stamp or Tourist/Residence Visa copy (if currently inside the UAE)
  • High-resolution passport-sized photograph (white background)
  • 3 Preferred company trade name choices in order of preference
  • Brief outline of intended commercial activities
